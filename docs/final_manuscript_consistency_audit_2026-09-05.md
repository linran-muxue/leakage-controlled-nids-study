# Final manuscript consistency audit (2026-09-05)

## Scope

This audit checks the final English and Chinese manuscript sources against the locked machine-readable result files. It checks numerical values that are explicitly reported in the manuscripts, formula presence, referenced figure/table files, and known interpretation boundaries. It does not replace a visual inspection of the final PDF produced by the journal submission system.

## Formula checks

- Random-forest equal soft voting is defined as (p(c|x)=T^{-1}\sum_i p_i(c|x)).
- Chi-square filtering is defined as (chi_j^2=\sum_c (O_{jc}-E_{jc})^2/E_{jc}), with the implementation caveat that the non-negative matrix is supplied to scikit-learn's filter score and is not interpreted causally.
- Validation-weighted voting is defined as (w_i=(b_i+\varepsilon)/\sum_j(b_j+\varepsilon)) and (p_w(c|x)=\sum_i w_i p_i(c|x)).
- Macro-F1 is defined as the arithmetic mean of per-class F1 scores.

## Interpretation checks

The manuscripts explicitly state that the CIC result is for a 3,365-row balanced research subset, not the full CIC-IDS2017 archive; the file-level experiment is a coverage/generalization-risk audit rather than a complete temporal test; NSL-KDD and UNSW-NB15 are independent benchmarks rather than direct transfer tests; the weighted forest is not claimed to be significantly better than equal voting; and offline prediction latency is not presented as end-to-end gateway deployment.
